Helped onboard 3 new engineers remotely this quarter.

What helped:
- Async walkthroughs via Loom
- “Open calendar” hours for questions
- Pair programming sessions

Still tricky:
- Lack of hallway conversations
- Understanding team culture

Tip: Assign a buddy. It makes all the difference.
