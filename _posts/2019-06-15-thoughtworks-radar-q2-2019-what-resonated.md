Q2 2019 Radar called out some interesting trends:
- Rise of `pulumi` as code-first infrastructure tool
- "Enclave Computing" for secure computation (a bit niche, but promising)
- Growing recognition of "event storming" for service design

We explored Pulumi briefly and liked the native language approach vs. Terraform DSL.

Event storming also helped clarify our domain models in backend services.
