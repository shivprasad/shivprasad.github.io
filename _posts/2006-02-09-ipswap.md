IPswap is the new community which allows unparalleled collaboration
between users and developers of software embedded products. In the
Participation Age, there are no arbitrary distinctions between users
and developers, manufactures and buyers. Be one, be both, be everything
in between. IPswap works with licensed software products, free software
products and with open source software products. IPswap is missing link
between a single source operation and a global collaborative world by
creating a market place for the participation age. The internet is the
medium, corporations create the products, and the people decide their
fate. Power to the People, to the Products, and to Entrepreneurship!

![](http://www.ipswap.com/images/ipswap/ipswapdiagram.gif)

IPswap will empower and unleash the creative skills of
software developers around the world who wishes to monetize their
efforts and deliver something of real value to the customer, without
the constraints imposed by existing software market practices.
