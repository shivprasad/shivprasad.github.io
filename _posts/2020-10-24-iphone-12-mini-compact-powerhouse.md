The iPhone 12 Mini was a breath of fresh air in a market filled with large phones.

- Same A14 chip as the Pro models
- Lightweight and truly one-hand usable
- 5G and OLED in a compact frame

Downsides?
- Battery life could be better
- MagSafe accessories still feel early

But for those of us craving smaller phones — this hits the sweet spot.
