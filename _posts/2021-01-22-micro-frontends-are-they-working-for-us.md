We’ve had a year of building with micro-frontends.

What worked:
- Team autonomy increased
- Code ownership clearer
- Deployments decoupled

What didn’t:
- Shared components drift
- CSS isolation issues
- Performance overhead (multiple bundles)

Still evolving — but here to stay in large orgs.
