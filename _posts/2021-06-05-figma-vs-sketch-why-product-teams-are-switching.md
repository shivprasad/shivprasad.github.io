Over the last year, we’ve gradually moved design collaboration to Figma.

Why Figma wins:
- Browser-based and real-time
- Easy to include engineers/product managers
- Plugins and community are thriving

Sketch still feels like a single-player tool. Figma is multiplayer by default.

Big win for hybrid and remote teams.
