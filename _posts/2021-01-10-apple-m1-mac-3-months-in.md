Been using the M1 MacBook Air for ~3 months now.

Thoughts:
- Performance is phenomenal. Everything feels snappy.
- Battery life is unmatched. I charge it once every two days.
- Rosetta 2 is magic — most apps just work.

Minor annoyances:
- Some Docker tooling still catching up
- Display support (multiple external monitors) is limited

This chip reset the bar for laptops.
