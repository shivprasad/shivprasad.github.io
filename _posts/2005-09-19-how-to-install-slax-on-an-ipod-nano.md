If you like Linux and you like the iPod nano, then why not slap a copy of [Slax](http://slax.linux-live.org/) on your nano and have a full OS with you wherever you go. Slax lets you run Linux directly from a CD / USB flash drive without installing anything on the computer you boot off of it. Better yet, [jakemikey has written a thorough how-to](http://slax.linux-live.org/forum/viewtopic.php?p=36718) walking you through the steps necessary to get Slax running smoothly on your iPod nano.
