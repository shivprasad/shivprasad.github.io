Monitoring tells you **when** something’s wrong. Observability tells you **why**.

Core pillars:
- Metrics
- Logs
- Traces

Tooling like Prometheus + Grafana, ELK, and Zipkin or Jaeger are key.

Start with structured logs — everything else grows from there.
