A new version of MSN Messenger is one of the projects currently being worked on
by the [Windows Live](http://arstechnica.com/news.ars/post/20051101-5512.html) team. The beta version, ostensibly called MSN
Messenger 8 but referred to in many places as "Windows Live Messenger," has so
far been available for use only by invitation. Beta test users could send e-mail
invites to their friends, but there was no way for the general public to test
the service.  

But [this](http://ideas.live.com/signup.aspx?versionId=0eccd94b-eb48-497c-8e60-c6313f7ebb73) link will take you to the invitation acceptance page. ;)
