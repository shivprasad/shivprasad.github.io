We once chased “velocity” like it was a stock ticker. Then came the sprints with 40 story points and little actual progress.

2014 taught me a key lesson: **velocity is not productivity**.

Real metrics:
- Time to recover from failure
- Percentage of deploys without rollback
- Reduction in onboarding friction for new devs

![Agile Metrics Diagram](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:720/format:webp/1*acZJYOq_QOJsoOJ5AqbsYg.png)

Agile Metrics Diagram

Measure what matters. Ignore what feels busy but means nothing.
