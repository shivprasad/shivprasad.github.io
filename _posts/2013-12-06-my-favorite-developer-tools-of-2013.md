2013 was a year of polishing my toolchain. I embraced minimal, fast tools that stayed out of the way.

Top picks:
- **iTerm2** with [oh-my-zsh](https://ohmyz.sh/)
- **Alfred App** for clipboard, launchers, and workflows
- **Dash** for offline API docs
- **Mavericks’ Xcode** for just being stable for once

![Alfred Screenshot](https://i.imgur.com/8JKh3A8.png)

Alfred Screenshot

It wasn’t about fancy IDEs. It was about tools that respected my time.
