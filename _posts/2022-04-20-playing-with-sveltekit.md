Decided to try out SvelteKit for a hobby project.

Pros:
- Fast and minimal
- Built-in routing and transitions are slick
- Great DX, smaller learning curve than React

I don’t think it’s dethroning React yet, but it’s got real potential — especially for smaller apps.
