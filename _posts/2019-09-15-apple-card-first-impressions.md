Got my Apple Card this month. Impressions?

- The titanium card is a beauty
- UX inside Apple Wallet is miles ahead of anything else
- Real-time notifications, budgeting tools, no fees

Apple’s tight hardware-software integration shines here. It's not just a card, it’s a service.

Only downside: not accepted everywhere due to Goldman Sachs backend and Mastercard limitations.
