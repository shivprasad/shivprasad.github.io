Google Calendar

Google on Thursday plans to unveil a much-anticipated, free Web-based calendar application that is expected to heat up the race with Yahoo and Microsoft. The free Web-based [Google Calendar](http://dw.com.com/redir?destUrl=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.google.com%2Fcalendar&siteId=3&oId=2100-1032-6060741&ontId=1023&lop=nl.ex), allows users to build online calendars that enable specific individuals or groups of people to access all or some of the events listed. A Gmail account is not necessary to use Google Calendar.
