WWDC 2020 was full of surprises, but the biggest was Apple’s move away from Intel.

Highlights:
- ARM-based Apple Silicon for Mac
- Rosetta 2 to translate Intel apps
- Developer transition kit already available

For devs:
- Start testing builds on ARM
- Universal Binaries are back
- Performance-per-watt changes everything

This marks the beginning of a new era for the Mac.
