Are you tired of wondering if your finely crafted interface will look correct in Safarai or the latest Konqueror on Linux? If so, check out [browsershots.](http://browsershots.org/) Just enter the URL you want screenshots for and it gets added to the queue. Check back (the delay is around a 12 hours lately) and you're screenshots will be posted.
