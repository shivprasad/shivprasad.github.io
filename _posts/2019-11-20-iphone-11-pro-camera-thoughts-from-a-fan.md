Got my hands on the iPhone 11 Pro — and as expected, the camera is brilliant.

Night Mode is a game-changer. Low light performance finally rivals standalone cameras.

The ultra-wide lens is fun, though edge distortion is real.

Best part? Photos in HEIF format save space without loss in visible quality.

This might be the first phone where I didn’t reach for my DSLR all month.
