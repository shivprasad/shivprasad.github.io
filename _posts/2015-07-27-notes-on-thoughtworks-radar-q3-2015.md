This quarter’s [Radar](https://www.thoughtworks.com/radar) highlights:
- **Immutable infrastructure** is no longer just theory — Docker adoption is pushing it mainstream
- **Contract testing** continues to evolve with tools like Pact
- Observability and automation dominate “Adopt” and “Trial” rings

My picks:
- `Adopt`: Feature toggles as default
- `Trial`: Chaos engineering tools
- `Hold`: Shared dev DBs — still a no

Radar gives shape to gut instincts. Always worth a read.
