As an Apple fanboy, September means one thing: new iPhones. The XS and XS Max were unveiled this time — sporting A12 Bionic chips and gorgeous OLED displays.

The focus was performance-per-watt, neural engine enhancements, and camera smarts.

My favorite part? The deep integration between hardware and software. Apple continues to push vertical optimization — and it shows.

As usual, I followed the keynote with the same awe I had watching the original iPhone in 2007.
