- Install the [URLid](http://extensionroom.mozdev.org/more-info/uriid) Mozilla/Firefox extension.
- Download [this CSS file](http://persistent.info/files/20041005.gmail.css).
- Locate your [profile folder](http://www.mozilla.org/products/firefox/support/edit.html#profile) and the `chrome` folder within that.
- Copy the downloaded CSS file to the `chrome` folder and rename it to `userContent.css` (if you already have such a file, you will have to merge the two).
- Restart Firefox.
- Visit Gmail.
