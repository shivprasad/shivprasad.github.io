Tried Windows 11 via Insider Preview — surprisingly polished.

- Centered taskbar and new icons feel very macOS-inspired
- Snap Layouts are a productivity win
- WSLg support is a game changer for devs

Still some rough edges with legacy UI, but it's a bold reboot. Curious how enterprise adoption plays out.
