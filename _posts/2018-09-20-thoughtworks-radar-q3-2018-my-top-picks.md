- Serverless as a first-class citizen
- GitOps practices maturing
- Rise of developer-centric observability tools (Lightstep, Honeycomb)

The move toward automation and autonomy for teams was clear.

As someone pushing for strong infra ownership across teams, these trends validated many of our bets.
