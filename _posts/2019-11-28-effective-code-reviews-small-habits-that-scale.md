Our code review process became much healthier this quarter.

What helped:
- Clear review checklist (naming, error handling, logging)
- Encouraged pairing on complex PRs
- Avoided nit-picking (let formatters handle that)

Most impactful change? Assigning reviewers based on ownership — not just who’s free.

It made engineers more accountable and reviews more thoughtful.
