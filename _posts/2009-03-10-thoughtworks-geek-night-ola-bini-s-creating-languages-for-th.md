Last night, I had a privilege of attending [Ola Bini](http://olabini.com/)'s session on "Language on JVM" at ThoughtWorks UK office. It was a two hours of geeky stuff, trust me. Most of the stuff went over my head, but I was able to grasp some of it.
He also provided glimpse of [Ioke](http://ioke.org/) language he is working on. I really liked the part where he demoed [Conditions](http://ioke.org/wiki/index.php/Guide#Conditions), the way of handling exceptions in Ioke, quite different than other languages.. kinda cool :)
Here is a [link](http://olabini.com/presentations/CreatingALanguageForTheJVM.pdf) to the pdf if you are interested.
