Budget iPhone is back.

The SE 2020 feels like a sleeper hit:
- Same A13 chip as iPhone 11
- Classic design (TouchID!)
- Solid battery life and camera

Downsides:
- Bezels feel dated in 2020
- No night mode

Still — for $399 — it's the phone I'd recommend to most people.
