Barry wanted a special and original wedding proposal. Guess what he did? He approached [Ask Jeeves](http://www.ask.com) to make a special arrangement for him.

When he asked her girlfriend, Yisha Tversky to search on [her name](http://web.ask.com/web?q=yisha+tversky), she saw the "Yisha, will you marry me?" message to display on Ask Jeeves. And while she was doing that, Barry kneeled behind her with the ring and flowers.
