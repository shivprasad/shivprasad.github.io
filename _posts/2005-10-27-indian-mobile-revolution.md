According to a study by [Research and Markets](http://www.researchandmarkets.com/reports/c23622/), the Indian mobile handset market is now worth about $2 billion, but will surge by over 60 percent in two years. Nokia dominates the market with a 59 percent market share, followed by Samsung with 13 percent and Motorola with 7 percent.
