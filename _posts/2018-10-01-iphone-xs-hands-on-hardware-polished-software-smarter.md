After watching the launch, I finally got my hands on the iPhone XS.

Impressions:
- OLED screen is stunning — deep blacks and sharp contrast
- Face ID noticeably faster than iPhone X
- A12 Bionic feels like overkill in everyday tasks, but future-ready

Camera processing is where the magic really shows — Smart HDR and depth control are standout features.

As always, Apple nailed the hardware-software integration.
