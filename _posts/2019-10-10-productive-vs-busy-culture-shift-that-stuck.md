One of the key shifts we made this year — stop celebrating being "busy."

Instead of:
- "Worked late all week"

We now ask:
- "What outcome did we achieve?"

Weekly demos and async updates helped keep focus on progress, not effort.

It’s amazing how the language we use shapes the culture.
