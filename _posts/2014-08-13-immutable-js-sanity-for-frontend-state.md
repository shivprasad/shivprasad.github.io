[ThoughtWorks Radar (July 2014)](https://www.thoughtworks.com/radar/languages-and-frameworks/immutable-js) listed **Immutable.js** — and it was a turning point for how I thought about frontend state.

No more mutated state bugs. No more complex diffing logic. Immutable structures made UI behavior **predictable**.

![Immutable.js Concept](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/2e/Immutable_logo.svg/1024px-Immutable_logo.svg.png)

Immutable.js Concept

This laid the foundation for better Redux architecture and **side-effect-free UIs** in the years ahead.
