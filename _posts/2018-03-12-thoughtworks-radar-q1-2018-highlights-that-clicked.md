Some of my takeaways from the ThoughtWorks Radar Q1 2018:
- `Kubernetes Operators` moved into “Trial”
- `Pulumi` and `Serverless Framework` gaining steam
- “Team Topologies” style thinking entering tech discourse

I was already experimenting with K8s Operators and found the pattern surprisingly elegant — especially for managing CRDs declaratively.

Also, Pulumi’s approach of using general-purpose languages for infra-as-code intrigued me. It felt natural for developers to adopt.

Radar continues to be a valuable pulse on emerging tech.
