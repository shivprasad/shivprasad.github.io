As teams rapidly shifted to remote work this quarter, some things worked better than expected.

What helped:
- Async updates on Slack instead of endless Zoom
- Virtual whiteboarding with Miro
- Keeping team rituals but shortening the duration

Mistakes:
- Over-scheduling early weeks
- No defined "offline" hours

We’re adapting fast — and some habits (like clearer documentation) are keepers.
