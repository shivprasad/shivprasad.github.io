Videora 2.0 is the latest version of new personal video downloading
program. Utilizing BitTorrent peer to peer technology and Really Simple
Syndication (RSS) feeds, Videora automatically and intelligently finds
and downloads video you want to watch. With easy to use features like
Watch Lists and Season Tickets you will be able to watch your favorite
video, no matter where you are in the world. All you need to get
started is a broadband internet connection and Windows.
