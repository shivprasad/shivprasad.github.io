While in no way a secret, [Google Base](http://base.google.com/) has been given the greenlight (at least light green, it’s beta afterall). Search Engine Watch [has a nice roundup](http://blog.searchenginewatch.com/blog/051116-000001) that helps define what Base really is.

> Google Base is a place where you can add all types of information that we’ll host and make searchable online.
> 

> You can describe any item you post with attributes, which will help people find it when they search Google Base. In fact, based on the relevance of your items, they may also be included in the main Google search index and other Google products like Froogle and Google Local.
> 

[Visit](http://base.google.com)
