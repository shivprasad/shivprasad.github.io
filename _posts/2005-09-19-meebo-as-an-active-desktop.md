Meebo recently updated their service to support Jabber/ Google Talk. Meebo is an AJAX web based chat service which support AIM/ICQ, MSN, Yahoo, and now Jabber.

If youâ€™re on Windows and wish to use Meebo as your Active Desktop, follow the steps below:

1. Right-click on your desktop and go to **Properties > Desktop**
2. Click the **Customize Desktop** button. In the resulting window navigate to the **Web** tab.  
3. Click **New**. In the *New Desktop Item* window enter http://meebo.com in the **Location** field and click **OK**.
