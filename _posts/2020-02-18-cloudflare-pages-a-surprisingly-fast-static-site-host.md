I moved one of my static sites to [Cloudflare Pages](https://pages.cloudflare.com).

Reasons:
- Integrated CDN and edge caching
- Git-based deploys
- Free tier generous enough

Compared with Netlify, Pages is faster to render from edge locations.

Perfect fit for blog archives and tools with no backend dependencies.
