The Indian government is funding an initiative to distribute free CDs containing open-source software. Around 3.5 million CDs of Tamil-language versions of open-source applications and 3.5 million Hindi-language CDs containing have already been sent out. There are plans to distribute software translated into all 22 official languages of India.[Visit](http://news.com.com/India+Speaking+your+language/2100-7344_3-5951942.html?part=rss&tag=5951942&subj=news)
