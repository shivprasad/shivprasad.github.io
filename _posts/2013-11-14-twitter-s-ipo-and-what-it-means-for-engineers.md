[Twitter IPO'd](https://www.cnbc.com/id/101175746) this month. Beyond the headlines, I found myself reflecting on what this means for engineers.

Twitter’s scale challenges are legendary—event queues, data pipelines, fanout models. Engineers there helped define how we think about **real-time systems**.

It’s a reminder that behind every billion-dollar valuation, there's infrastructure that’s often invisible. And engineers who had to fix things at 3 a.m.

If we want sustainable tech companies, we need to celebrate *technical stewardship* as much as product vision.
