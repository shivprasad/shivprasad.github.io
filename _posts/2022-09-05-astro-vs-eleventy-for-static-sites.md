Rebuilding a small docs site gave me a chance to compare:

**Astro:**
- Island architecture
- Framework-agnostic
- Larger bundle size

**Eleventy:**
- Simpler config
- Faster build for smaller sites

Verdict: Astro wins for component-rich sites. Eleventy for speed and simplicity.
